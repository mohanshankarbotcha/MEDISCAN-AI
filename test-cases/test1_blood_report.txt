ADDITIONAL NOTES:
Iron deficiency anemia is the most common nutritional deficiency worldwide.
Characterized by low hemoglobin, low serum ferritin, elevated TIBC.
Treatment typically involves oral iron supplementation 100-200mg elemental
iron daily for 3-6 months with dietary modification to include iron-rich foods
such as leafy greens, lean red meat, legumes, and fortified cereals.
Follow-up CBC and iron studies recommended after 8 weeks of treatment.
Vitamin C supplementation aids iron absorption. Avoid tea and coffee
within 1 hour of iron supplement intake as tannins inhibit absorption.
